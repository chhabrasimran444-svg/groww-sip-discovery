SIP Lens

An AI SIP analyser for every SIP: red, green, skipped or cancelled. It explains why the SIP moved, what the investor has built and what pausing or stopping would change. It explains; it never predicts or advises. Not the Groww app and not affiliated with Groww.

Demo controls
1. Dashboard: every SIP has a SIP Lens chip, red or green. 2. SIPs tab: active, skipped and cancelled SIPs each have a SIP Lens button. 3. The lens icon at the top (hover: “SIP analyser”) lists every SIP to review. 4. Try Manage SIP → Stop SIP to see “Before you stop”.

Data: real daily NAVs of one fund per category (AMFI data via mfapi.in), fetched live. Fund names are hidden so nothing reads as a recommendation. Each demo SIP invests a fixed amount on the 5th of every month; amounts and start dates are illustrative. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks.
